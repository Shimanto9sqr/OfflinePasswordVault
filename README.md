# Password Vault 🔒

**Your Private & Encrypted Password Manager**

A modern, secure, and offline-first Android application built with **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose** for managing your passwords with military-grade encryption and a beautiful **Material 3** design.

## ✨ Features

Secure Vault is designed for maximum security and ease of use, putting you in control of your data.

- **Offline & Completely Encrypted** 🔐: All your data is stored **locally** on your device and is encrypted end-to-end using **SQLCipher**. Your passwords never leave your device unencrypted.

- **Robust Security** 🗝️: Unlock the app using **Biometric authentication** (Fingerprint, Face Unlock) or your **Screen Lock Password** for quick and secure access.

- **Strong Password Generation** 🔑: Integrated **Password Generator** to create complex, unguessable passwords on demand.

- **Data Management** 💾: Securely **Import/Export** your entire encrypted database to back up your data or move it between devices.

- **Organization** 📂: Use **Categories** to effectively group and organize your saved passwords (e.g., Work, Social, Finance).

- **Efficient Navigation** ⏬: **Filter** your passwords based on their assigned Category.

- **Custom Ordering** 📃: Choose to sort your passwords by **Name** (A-Z) or by the **Last Updated** date.

- **Auto App Lock** 🔒: Automatically locks the application after a period of inactivity to ensure your data remains protected even if you forget to close the app.
